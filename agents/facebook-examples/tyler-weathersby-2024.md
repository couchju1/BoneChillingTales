HE KISSED HIS FAMILY GOODBYE. THEN HE WALKED AWAY AND VANISHED.
On the morning of September 4, 2024, 31-year-old Tyler Blake Weathersby kissed his wife goodbye outside their Sioux Falls, South Dakota, home.
His children were getting ready to leave for school. It was an ordinary family goodbye, the kind that happens in countless driveways every morning.
But Tyler never came home.
He left behind his phone, wallet, and keys.
And more than two years later, his family is still searching for answers.
Tyler was a husband, father, entrepreneur, and creative spirit. Alongside his wife, MonaLisa Perez, he helped build Wake, a clothing brand that grew into a creative agency.
He was also someone who enjoyed long walks.
According to his wife, walking helped him clear his mind. Sometimes he would be gone for an hour or two, so when he left that morning, there was initially little reason for concern.
But something had been different in the hours leading up to his disappearance.
The previous day, Tyler had argued with his mother, Janine Weathersby Harris. She later told NBC's Dateline that such an argument was completely out of character for their relationship.
His older brother, Gregory, also noticed something unusual when they spoke by phone. Tyler wasn't communicating the way he normally did.
Nobody knew what to make of it.
The following morning, Tyler went for an early walk, returned home, and then left for a second walk after his family departed.
That second walk would become the center of a missing-person investigation.
By approximately 10:30 a.m., his wife was worried. She searched the neighborhood and places Tyler frequently visited.
There was no sign of him.
Investigators eventually recovered surveillance footage showing Tyler walking alone toward the interstate.
Then another piece of evidence surfaced.
In February 2025, private investigators released dashcam footage reportedly showing Tyler near the intersection of West Madison Street and North Marion Road.
In the video, Tyler appears to speak with a boy riding a bicycle.
Moments later, he approaches a silver Hyundai Santa Fe.
What was said during those encounters remains unknown.
Authorities have not publicly established that either person was involved in his disappearance.
Police previously stated they had found no evidence of foul play, but Tyler's whereabouts remain a mystery.
His mother and wife have both expressed disbelief that he would intentionally harm himself.
Today, Tyler's family is still waiting for something most families take for granted.
An answer.
Tyler was last seen wearing a white T-shirt, black shorts, and a tan stocking hat. He is approximately 6 feet tall, weighs 155 pounds, and has a crown tattoo with a halo on his right wrist.
**Someone may remember seeing Tyler that September morning. Someone may know where his second walk ended.**
