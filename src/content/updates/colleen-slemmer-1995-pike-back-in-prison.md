Christa Pike has been released from the hospital and moved back to prison, her lawyers said on October 10, 2026. She will keep getting medical care there. [S12] She left the hospital, not state custody.

Pike was sentenced to death for killing 19-year-old Colleen Slemmer in Knoxville in 1995. [S1] Tennessee tried to execute her on September 30, 2026. She survived two doses of pentobarbital and was taken to a hospital, where she was put on a ventilator. [S6, S12]

Everything known about her recovery comes from her lawyers, not from medical records. On October 8 they said she was walking with some help, eating a regular diet, and regaining movement in her arms. Doctors cautioned it could be months before anyone knows whether she has lasting cognitive injuries. [S10]

On October 7, a judge ordered the state to preserve the IV lines, syringes and other evidence from the attempt. [S11] An independent review led by former U.S. attorney Ed Stanton is looking at whether the prison system followed its execution protocol. [S12] Her lawyers have asked Governor Bill Lee to commute her sentence to life without parole. The state has not said whether it will try to execute her again. [S11, S12]
