On Wednesday, October 28, the Black Hawk County Sheriff's Office charged Lindell Andre Jackson Sr., 50, of Waterloo, with first-degree murder in the death of Angie Buck. He was already in jail on an unrelated case. [S11, S12]

According to the complaint, as KCRG and KWWL describe it, Jackson was at the house on Cottage Street that night with Angie and Kelly Grant. [S11, S12] In a 1996 interview, he denied being in Waterloo that weekend. [S12] No plea has been reported.

Grant, charged in mid-October, has been brought back from Minnesota and is held in the Black Hawk County Jail. [S11, S12] He has denied any part in her death. [S5]

Both men are presumed innocent unless proven guilty.
