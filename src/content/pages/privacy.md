Last updated October 9, 2026.

Bone Chilling Tales is a website about real crimes and unsolved cases. You can read every story without an account, and we keep what we collect about you small. This page explains what that is.

## The short version

- We don't sell your information, and we don't share it for advertising.
- We don't have accounts, comments or logins.
- The newsletter gets your email address only if you sign up.
- The suggestion form sends what you type to our inbox. It isn't stored on the website.
- Analytics tells us which stories people read. You can turn it off, and we honor Global Privacy Control.

## What we collect and why

**Newsletter.** If you sign up, your email address goes to Kit (formerly ConvertKit), the service that sends our weekly email. If you signed up from a story, Kit may also note which story it was, so we can tell you when that case changes. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops the emails, and you can ask us to delete your address completely.

**Suggest a case or a correction.** The form sends us an email with what you type: the case, the town, your message, any links, and your email address if you give one. We use it to look into the case or fix the mistake, and to reply if you asked us to. We don't publish what you send or share your email address. To stop spam, the site keeps a scrambled code made from your internet address for up to an hour, to limit how many messages one connection can send. It can't be turned back into your address.

**Analytics.** We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which stories people read, how far they read, and whether they sign up for the newsletter or tap an agency's tip line. Google sets cookies to do this and receives your internet address, browser type and the pages you visit. We don't send Google your name or email address. Analytics loads only after the page finishes, and:

- Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland start with analytics cookies turned off.
- If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we don't load analytics at all.
- The "Turn off analytics" link at the bottom of every page turns it off in your browser. The choice is saved in your browser, not on our server.

**Server logs.** Our web host, Hostinger, keeps standard logs of requests to the site, like your internet address, browser and the page you asked for. These are used for security and to keep the site running.

## Other services that load on some pages

**YouTube.** Video thumbnails load from YouTube's image server, which sees your internet address. The video player itself loads only when you tap play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). After that, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

**Links to other sites.** Stories link to news reports, court records, agencies and archives. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Our fonts and images are stored on our own server, so loading a page doesn't contact a font service or image service.

## Cookies and browser storage

We don't set cookies ourselves. Google Analytics sets cookies when it's turned on. If you turn analytics off, the site saves that choice in your browser's local storage so it remembers next time.

## Ads

There are no ads on the site right now. If we add them, we'll update this page first, and ads will come with a way to make choices about ad tracking.

## Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has sent us information, tell us and we'll delete it.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, state or national privacy laws may give you more rights. We'll honor the request either way. We don't sell personal information or share it for cross-context advertising.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
