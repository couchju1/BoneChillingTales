In Nebraska, the State Patrol runs a cold case unit that reviews unsolved homicides for any agency in the state, often looking for evidence that newer DNA testing can read. Omaha police have their own cold case unit as well.

The Patrol also prints decks of playing cards with unsolved Nebraska murders on them and hands them out in jails and prisons, hoping someone recognizes a face. Each story below lists the agency to call if you know something.
