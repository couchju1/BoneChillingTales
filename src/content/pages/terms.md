By using Bone Chilling Tales, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

## What the stories are

The stories here are journalism about real cases, written from public records, court filings and news reports. They're for information. They aren't legal advice, and they aren't the final word on any case. Cases change: people are charged, charges are dropped, convictions are overturned. Each story shows when we last checked it, and the sources are listed so you can read them yourself.

We're not law enforcement and we're not connected to any agency. **If you have information about a crime, contact the agency handling the case, or call 911 if someone is in danger.** Don't send tips to us.

Someone charged with a crime is presumed innocent unless a court convicts them. When a story says a person was "accused" or "charged," that's all it means.

## Mistakes

We work to get every fact right, and we fix mistakes when we find them. Our [standards page](/standards/) explains how we report and correct stories, and every fix is listed on the [corrections page](/corrections/). If you see an error, [tell us](/suggest/).

## Sharing our work

You're welcome to link to any story and to quote a few sentences with a link back to the story. Please don't copy whole stories or large parts of them, republish them elsewhere, or use them to train or feed automated systems without our written permission.

The words and design of this site belong to Bone Chilling Tales. Photos, documents and quotes from other sources belong to their owners and are credited where they appear.

## If you think we used your work wrongly

If you own a photo or other material on this site and believe we used it without permission, contact us with the page, the material and your contact details. We'll look into it and respond within 48 hours.

## What you send us

When you use the suggestion form, you let us use what you send in our reporting, such as looking into a case or fixing a mistake. We won't publish your name or email address without your permission. Please don't send anything you don't have the right to share, and don't send confidential details about an open investigation.

Family members can ask us to correct a story or remove a photo. We'll answer personally.

## Links to other sites

Stories link to outside websites like news outlets, court records and agencies. We don't control those sites and aren't responsible for what's on them.

## Using the site

Please don't try to break into the site, overload it, or use automated tools to copy large parts of it. We may block access that does.

## No guarantees

We provide the site as it is. We try to keep it accurate and running, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of errors. To the extent the law allows, Bone Chilling Tales isn't responsible for losses that come from using the site or relying on it.

## Changes

We may update these terms. If we do, we'll change the date at the top of this page. Using the site after an update means you accept the new terms.
