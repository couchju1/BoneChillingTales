## The hard rules for this video

- **Midjourney and Higgsfield make atmosphere only.** No people, faces, hands, silhouettes, figures in the distance, dorms or a party, a search, divers, boats, police, a cap, a computer screen, documents, maps or anything that could pass for the real campus.
- **Never run a photo of Josh through Higgsfield, Midjourney or any face tool.** No "animate this portrait", no lip sync, no aging. Photos of Josh move only by a slow pan or zoom in the editor.
- **Never show the 28 images the sheriff released.** Not blurred, not cropped, not in a thumbnail.
- **No music sting or dramatic pause on a named person's line** (Brian, Lisa, Paul, Nick, the sheriffs, the investigators). Keep the music bed level through quotes.
- **No AI voice for real people.** One narrator reads every quote, introduced with "he said" as written. Don't clone or imitate Brian, Lisa, Paul or anyone else.

## Midjourney settings

Paste the prompt text, then the parameters.

- Long video: `--ar 16:9 --style raw --stylize 50 --no people, person, figure, silhouette, face, hands, text, letters, logo, sign, building, boat --v 7`
- Short: same, with `--ar 9:16`
- If your account defaults to a newer version, drop `--v 7`.
- Keep the seed of the first image you like (`--seed [number]`) and reuse it so the set looks like one place.
- Reject any image where Midjourney sneaks in a figure, a dock with a person, a building, or lettering. Reroll it.

## Higgsfield settings

Image to video, on the approved Midjourney stills only. 5 second clips, then slow them to 60 to 80 percent in the editor if they feel rushed. Camera moves: slow dolly in, slow pan, or static with drifting mist. Add to every motion prompt: `no people appear, nothing enters the frame, no text`. Reject any clip where something appears or the scene changes into a place.
