# Bone Chilling Tales: brand voice

## Who we are
Bone Chilling Tales (bonechillingtales.com) tells the full stories of real crimes and unsolved
cases, with every source listed. Tagline: "True crime stories that stay with you." The site is
run by Justin Couch, founder and editor. Real cases only. No fiction, legends or ghost stories.

## Audience
Adults who follow true crime podcasts and documentaries. They read on their phones, often at
night. They trust a site that shows its sources and spot hype and lazy AI writing right away.

## Voice
- Calm and exact. The facts carry the weight, so the writing doesn't need to.
- Plain words and short sentences. Tension comes from the order you tell things in, not from
  adjectives.
- Confident about what's documented, honest about what isn't. "Police have never said" beats
  guessing.
- Warm toward victims and their families. They are people first, not plot points.
- No jokes about real cases.

## Always
- Start with a specific person, place and date.
- Tie every fact to a source.
- Say who claims something when it's disputed: "prosecutors allege", "her family says".
- Use legal terms correctly. "Charged with" or "accused" until convicted. Say so near the top
  if charges were dropped or someone was acquitted.
- End with where the case stands now.

## Never
- Invent facts, quotes, dates, names or details.
- Name someone as a suspect unless official records or court filings call them one.
- Hint that anyone who wasn't charged did it.
- Describe injuries or deaths in more detail than the story needs.
- Name minors accused of crimes (unless charged as adults and named by the court) or sexual
  assault victims (unless they chose to speak publicly).
- Use crime scene photos, private family photos, or Midjourney images of real people.
- Promise something in a headline the story doesn't deliver.

## Banned words and punctuation
The site's build fails on these, so never use them:
delve, elevate, empower, seamless, unlock, robust, leverage, streamline, comprehensive,
cutting-edge, top-notch, one-stop shop, tailored solutions, passionate about, trusted partner,
we pride ourselves, journey, look no further, in today's fast-paced world, whether you're,
bombshell, shocking, chilling turn, little did they know, sent shockwaves, tight-knit community,
a cold case that haunts.

Also avoid: spine-tingling, buckle up, tapestry, in the realm of, it's important to note,
stacks of three adjectives.

No em dashes, en dashes or semicolons. Use a period or a comma instead.

## Formatting
- Short paragraphs, 1 to 3 sentences.
- Facebook: no headers, [0 to 1] emoji per post, [3 to 5] hashtags, always #BoneChillingTales.
- Website: no emoji, no hashtags.
