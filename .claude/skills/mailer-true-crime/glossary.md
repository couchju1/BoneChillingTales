# Glossary

**Absent Author**: the narrator never comments, interprets, or moralizes; the author speaks only in an afterword (Ch 1, 10)
**Aftermath Through Witnesses**: discovery and response told by bystanders in their idiom (Ch 6)
**Biography Burst**: compact life-history passage when a person enters (Ch 5)
**Block**: older term in this skill for a numbered section (Ch 2)
**Borrowed Voice**: narration takes on the diction of the viewpoint character (Ch 1)
**Chorus**: the many witnesses who tell the story collectively (Ch 3)
**Coda of Fates**: closing run of short sections tracing each survivor's next year (Ch 2, 8)
**Countdown**: minute-by-minute final movement to the execution (Ch 8)
**Cross-cutting**: alternating sections among people on the same day to build dread (Ch 2)
**Declarative Drive**: short subject-verb-object sentences in simple past (Ch 1)
**Dilation**: slowing narrative time to near real time at crisis points (Ch 2)
**Documentary Layer**: reproduced letters, transcripts, reports, wire copy (Ch 4)
**Eastern Voices**: Book Two; the media, legal, and national world; a more sophisticated register (Ch 8)
**Floe**: a single paragraph set off by white space, median ~37 words (Ch 2)
**Free Indirect Discourse (FID)**: untagged third-person thought in the character's own idiom (Ch 3)
**Functional Landscape**: place shown only as used or traveled (Ch 9)
**Interpretation by Proxy**: analysis given to a character, never the narrator (Ch 3)
**Introduce Through the Meeter**: a newcomer's first portrait filtered through whoever meets them (Ch 3)
**Material Specificity**: character through wages, cars, prices, possessions (Ch 5)
**Numbered Section**: 1, 2, 3… units inside titled chapters, one scene or viewpoint each (Ch 2)
**Opaque Center**: the killer kept partly unknowable, seen through others and documents (Ch 3)
**Ordinary Evening Approach**: normal life in the hours before the crime (Ch 6)
**Procedural Present**: strict-sequence flat narration of the act (Ch 6)
**Room Inventory**: cold, evidence-like listing of the murder room at the moment of the act (Ch 6)
**Script Transcript**: date/time header, SPEAKER in caps, indented speech; no Q/A (Ch 4)
**Single Detail**: one small concrete detail carrying a scene's weight (Ch 6)
**Source-Anchored Interiority**: every attributed thought traceable to a source (Ch 10)
**Story-Buyer**: the rights-purchasing producer as a viewpoint on how true crime gets made (Ch 8)
**True Life Story**: Mailer's afterword term for a factual account told with a novelist's technique (Ch 10)
**Two-Book Architecture**: local private story, then public spectacle (Ch 2)
**Victim Braiding**: victims' ordinary lives woven in before the crime (Ch 2, 5)
**Western Voices**: Book One; the plain local idiom of Utah family, lovers, cops (Ch 1, 2)
**White Space**: the blank gap between paragraphs and sections, used in place of transitions (Ch 2)
**Witness Relay**: rapid rotation of observers at the climax (Ch 3, 6, 8)
