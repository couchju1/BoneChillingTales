# Book Map: How *The Executioner's Song* Is Built

Taken from the full text (~355k words of narrative plus the afterword). Use it as a structural template. Percentages are shares of the whole narrative.

**Frame**: epigraph verse → Book One → Book Two → the same verse reprised, slightly changed → "An Afterword" (method, sources, thanks).

## Book One: Western Voices (~49%)
| Part | Chapters | Share | What happens | Template lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One: Gary | The First Day, The First Week, The First Month | 6% | The cousin's childhood memory, prison letters, sponsorship; release, jobs, clumsy re-entry | Open on innocence and hope |
| Two: Nicole | The House in Spanish Fork, Nicole and Uncle Lee, Nicole on the River | 5% | The girlfriend's own history: abuse, early marriages, kids, drifting, mental hospital | Give the lover a full Part of her own |
| Three: Gary and Nicole | Gary and Pete, The Job, In Trouble with the Law, In-Laws, Ex-Husbands | 8% | Romance, drinking, fights, petty theft; she flees | Small offenses pile up, unremarked |

## Proportions to imitate
- First murder about **20%** in. Crimes plus arrest take only ~10%.
- Trial ~7%. The post-sentence struggle, about half the book.
- Final day plus execution ~10%. Aftermath ~5%.
- As the clock tightens, chapter titles turn to days ("T.G.I.F.," "Saturday," "Dawn").
- Chapter titles are plain nouns, liturgical or calendar time, or deadpan irony ("The Turkey Shoot" for the execution).
